A meeting recorder for Windows, Mac and Linux

Your meeting, typed out live, with a second opinion before it ends.

MidMeeting is an app for your computer. Press record during any call, Zoom, Meet, Teams or a phone on speaker, and the words appear on your screen as people say them. Next to the text, AI advisors you pick read along and speak up when something is worth catching: a claim that does not add up, a question nobody asked, a task nobody owned. When the call ends you get a summary and the to-do list. The recording stays on your computer, and nobody on the call sees a bot.

Three steps, and you never leave the meeting.

01

Press record.

MidMeeting records what your microphone hears and what comes out of your speakers. It works with any meeting app, because it never touches the meeting app. No bot joins the call, nothing to install in Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord.

02

Read as it happens.

Speech is turned into text on your own computer, a few seconds behind the speaker. Your lines are told apart from everyone else's automatically: your voice comes in through the microphone, theirs through the speakers.

03

Let the advisors weigh in.

The AI advisors you picked read the text as it appears and speak only when a sharp colleague would interrupt. Highlight any line to ask a question about it.

AI advisors that speak up only when it matters.

Before the call, choose who sits in with you: an engineer, a devil's advocate, a researcher, a note taker, or one you describe in your own words. We call them agents. Each one reads the live text and, every time someone finishes a sentence, decides whether it has something worth saying. Usually it stays quiet. When it does speak, you get a short card, forty words or fewer, with the reason.

Cards arrive three to five seconds after the sentence ends. Every card is based only on what was actually said.

Highlight a line. Ask about it.

Select any part of the text and type a question: is this true, what did they say about this earlier, give me a counter-argument. The answer appears in a thread attached to the line you picked. After the call, the summary tab writes the summary, the action items, or a full report you can save.

Bring your own brain.

For power users

Recording and the live text work on their own and need no account. The AI advisors, the questions and the summary need an AI model to think with, and MidMeeting does not sell you one. Instead you connect the one you already use, and pay that company directly, usually a few cents per meeting.

Three ways to connect: paste an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini or xAI (a key is a long password you get from their website); point MidMeeting at a model running on your own computer with LM Studio or Ollama, which costs nothing and sends nothing anywhere; or, if you already run a coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex, let it sit in the meeting itself through the agent bridge. The recording belongs to you, in the ordinary sense.

  • Local by defaultAudio, text and summaries stay in a folder on your disk. Turning speech into text happens on your own computer, never on a server.
  • Nothing leaves unless you say soOnly the AI company you choose ever sees the text, and only while the AI advisors are on. Run the AI on your own computer and nothing leaves at all.
  • A visible window, not a hidden overlayMidMeeting is an app on your screen. It is a thinking partner you can share, not a cheat sheet you hide.

The things it does not do.

It does not put names on people. It knows which lines are yours, because they came in through your microphone, and which lines are everyone else's. It does not try to guess who among them said what.

No bot joins your meeting. Nothing appears in the participant list. The audio is never sent to a transcription service.

It is not a team product. One licence covers up to three of your own machines. There is no shared workspace and nothing syncs.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI advisor, or agent?

A set of instructions given to an AI model, such as "you are a sceptical engineer, flag claims that do not add up". MidMeeting ships with four and lets you write your own. They read the meeting text and speak only when they have something worth saying.

Do I need an AI account to use it?

Not for recording and the live text. For the advisors, the questions and the summary you connect an AI model: a pay-as-you-go key from an AI company, or a free model running on your own computer. See Bring your own brain.

Does it work with Zoom, Meet, Teams?

Yes, and with anything else that plays sound on your computer, including a phone on speaker. It records what your machine hears and what your microphone picks up, so it never depends on the meeting app.

Do I need a GPU?

No. It runs on any modern computer, just slower. A recent NVIDIA, AMD or Apple silicon graphics chip makes the live text feel instant.

What do the AI advisors cost to run?

Whatever the AI company charges you directly. A typical hour-long call with two advisors costs cents on a mid-size model, and nothing on a model running on your own computer.

Is my API key safe?

It is stored in your operating system's keyring, the same place your browser keeps passwords. It is never written to a settings file and never logged.

Can the other people tell?

Nothing joins the call, so no. Whether you tell them is between you and them, and MidMeeting is designed to be a window you can turn around and show.
