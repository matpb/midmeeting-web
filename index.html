Three steps, and you never leave the meeting.

01

Press record.

MidMeeting captures your microphone and your system audio as two separate tracks. No bot joins the call, no plugin touches Zoom, Meet, Teams or Discord.

02

Read as it happens.

Whisper runs on your machine and every line is labelled You or Them from the track it came from. Exact, not a guess.

03

Let the room weigh in.

The agents you armed watch the transcript and speak only when a sharp colleague would interrupt. Highlight any line to ask about it.

Agents that chime in, and mostly stay quiet.

A moderator dedupes and rate-limits, so what reaches you is a card, forty words or fewer, with a reason.

The things it does not do.

It does not name speakers. Lines are You or Them, from the track they came in on. That is exact, and it is all it claims.

No bot joins your meeting. Nothing appears in the participant list. Nothing is uploaded to a transcription service.

It is not a team product. One licence covers up to three of your own machines. There is no shared workspace and nothing syncs.

The ones that actually get asked.

Does it work with Zoom, Meet, Teams?

Yes, and with anything else that plays sound on your computer, including a phone on speaker. It records what your machine hears and what your microphone picks up, so it never depends on the meeting app.

Do I need a GPU?

No. Whisper runs on the CPU too, slower. A recent NVIDIA, AMD or Apple silicon GPU makes the live transcript feel instant.

What does the copilot cost to run?

Whatever your provider charges, shown live in the app. A typical hour-long call with two agents costs cents on a mid-size model, and nothing on a local one.

Is my API key safe?

It is stored in your operating system's keyring, the same place your browser keeps passwords. It is never written to a settings file and never logged.

Can the other people tell?

Nothing joins the call, so no. Whether you tell them is between you and them, and MidMeeting is designed to be a window you can turn around and show.
