Privacy.

The desktop app

Audio, transcripts and summaries are written to a folder on your own disk and stay there. There is no account, no login and no server behind the product for them to travel to.

Transcription runs locally with whisper, on your GPU or CPU. Nothing about a meeting is sent anywhere by default.

If you turn the copilot on, the transcript text you send it goes to whichever provider you chose in settings: Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or an agent CLI on your own machine. Pick a local model or a local agent and nothing leaves your machine at all. Your API key is stored in your operating system's keyring, never in a settings file and never logged.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads nothing external and collects nothing. No analytics, no trackers, no fonts or scripts from anywhere but this domain.
